## Public Entry Checklist

Create one row only after the project owner verifies every item:

| Public ID | Approved display name | Approved portrait asset | Consent confirmed | Owner approval date | Removal status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _No entries_ | — | — | — | — | — |

For each approved entry:

- use an ID that does not expose private information;
- record the exact public display name requested by the supporter;
- reference only a portrait the project has permission to publish;
- confirm consent covers both the name and portrait;
- confirm any required adult or guardian permission outside this repository;
- remove EXIF and GPS metadata before committing the asset; and
- remove or update the public entry promptly when requested.

## Private Evidence Boundary

Do not store emails, phone numbers, addresses, payment identifiers,
transaction amounts, identity documents, private messages or consent evidence
in this public repository. Keep private evidence and the removal-request
contact in an owner-controlled system outside Git.
