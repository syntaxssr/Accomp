FAQ

A few things to know.

Honest answers before launch.

What is Accomp?

Accomp is a mobile app concept for planning outdoor trips together in one shared trip space.

Can everyone help plan the same trip?

The planned experience supports invited companions contributing to trip details. Exact controls require validation.

Can I use Accomp without a signal?

Offline readiness is part of the direction. Final maps and data availability have not been announced.

Who is Accomp for?

Hiking groups, backpackers, campers, weekend adventurers, and friends who want one clear plan.

Will Accomp be available on iOS and Android?

Platform availability is not yet confirmed.

When will Accomp launch?

A launch date has not been announced.
