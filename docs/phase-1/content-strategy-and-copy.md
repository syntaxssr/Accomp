## 1. Communication Goal

ภายในช่วงแรกของหน้า ผู้ชมต้องเข้าใจว่า:

1. Accomp เป็นแอปสำหรับวางแผน outdoor trip ร่วมกับคนอื่น
2. แอปรวม route, itinerary, shared gear และข้อมูลสำคัญไว้ด้วยกัน
3. Offline readiness คือเหตุผลที่ทำให้ Accomp น่าเชื่อถือเมื่อออกไปนอกสัญญาณ
4. ขั้นตอนต่อไปคือเข้าร่วม waitlist ไม่ใช่ดาวน์โหลดแอปที่ยังไม่ยืนยันว่าเปิดตัวแล้ว

## 2. Messaging Hierarchy

### Brand idea

**The trusted companion for every shared adventure.**

### Primary promise

**Adventure together.**

### Value proposition

Bring the route, the crew, the gear, and the plan into one calm place—so everyone can head out ready.

### Reasons to believe

- One shared trip space
- Collaborative itinerary
- Shared gear checklist
- Essential trip details
- Offline maps

All five reasons to believe require final product validation.

### Emotional outcome

Less time chasing details. More confidence in the people and the plan.

## 3. Audience Jobs

### Functional

- Create a trip without spreading information across chats and documents
- Invite people into one shared plan
- Decide who brings each essential item
- Keep the itinerary and key information easy to find
- Prepare maps before losing signal

### Emotional

- Feel prepared without feeling overwhelmed
- Trust that everyone knows the plan
- Reduce uncertainty at the trailhead
- Preserve the feeling of adventure rather than turning planning into admin

### Social

- Be the dependable friend who brings everyone together
- Help beginners feel included
- Make group preparation visible and fair

## 4. Voice Rules for This Page

- Use `we/you/your crew` sparingly and naturally
- Prefer concrete verbs: plan, invite, assign, pack, download, explore
- Prefer calm confidence over urgency
- Keep headlines short enough to read in one breath
- Explain benefits before technical behavior
- Never imply emergency-grade safety, guaranteed connectivity or verified adoption
