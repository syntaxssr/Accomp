# Accomp Visual Direction v1

> Concept: **The Shared Path**  
> Status: Directional draft — not a final UI design  
> Visual board: [PNG preview](./visual-direction-board.png) · [SVG source](./visual-direction-board.svg)

## 1. Creative Thesis

Accomp ไม่ควรดูเหมือนแอปแผนที่ที่เย็นและเป็นเครื่องมือเพียงอย่างเดียว และไม่ควรดูเหมือนแบรนด์ outdoor ที่ใช้อะดรีนาลีนเป็นจุดขาย

ภาพรวมควรสื่อ:

**Calm preparation makes shared adventure feel possible.**

จุดเด่นของภาพจึงอยู่ที่ “การเดินทางมาบรรจบกัน” มากกว่าภูเขา ป่าสน หรือ location pin แบบตรงตัว

## 2. Visual Signature — The Shared Path

### Core device

ใช้เส้นสองเส้นที่:

- เริ่มจากคนละตำแหน่ง
- เคลื่อนเข้าใกล้กัน
- เชื่อมเป็นเส้นทางเดียว
- เปลี่ยนรูปตามบริบทของแต่ละ section

### Transformations

| Section | Shared-path expression |
|---|---|
| Hero | Two routes converge behind a phone mockup |
| Plan Together | Route becomes a day-by-day itinerary |
| Pack Together | Route nodes become checklist states |
| Ready Anywhere | Route expands into topographic contours |
| How It Works | Three steps connect on one continuous line |
| Final CTA | Two companion dots leave the frame together |

### Recognition rule

ผู้ชมควรเห็นเพียงเส้นสองเส้น จุด companion สองจุด และ Trail Sage แล้วเชื่อมโยงกับ Accomp ได้ แม้ไม่มีภาพภูเขาหรือโลโก้

## 5. Visual Ingredients

### 5.1 Photography

#### Human behavior to capture

- Friends leaning over a map or phone together
- Dividing shared gear
- Packing at home or at a trailhead
- Checking a route before leaving signal
- Walking at an inclusive group pace
- Small moments of assistance and trust

#### Framing

- Candid rather than posed
- Medium and wide shots over tight influencer portraits
- Space in the frame for type or UI overlays
- Subjects placed off-center when the composition needs room for route graphics
- Eye-level perspective to feel like another companion, not a spectator

#### Light and grade

- Natural morning or late-afternoon light
- Real greens and earth tones
- Gentle contrast
- Skin tones remain natural
- Avoid teal-orange grading and heavy HDR

### 5.2 Product Mockups

- Use one primary phone at a time in hero
- Use partial crops to emphasize one task per feature card
- Present screens as helpful artifacts, not floating tech trophies
- Allow route lines and checklist nodes to pass behind or through the composition
- Avoid copying device arrangement from Phantom

### 5.3 Topographic Field

- Thin 1–2px contour lines
- Wide line spacing in calm areas
- Denser contours only around focal points
- Low contrast on light surfaces
- Meadow Sage on Brand Dark for the offline chapter
- No literal map data unless it is properly sourced and accurate

### 5.4 Companion Dots

Two rounded dots act as people without drawing literal characters:

- Primary dot: Trail Sage
- Secondary dot: Meadow Sage, Trail Sand or Warm Cream ตามพื้นหลัง
- Dots never bounce
- The distance between dots represents collaboration state
- When they meet, they continue as two visible companions rather than merging into one

### 5.5 Cards

- Large rounded rectangles
- One idea per card
- Short headline at the top
- Product crop or original illustration anchored low
- Minimal border and controlled shadow
- Use varied surface tone, not random rainbow colors

## 6. Palette Application

### Recommended proportion

- 55% Warm Cream / quiet light space
- 20% Trail Sand and light surface cards
- 15% Brand Dark / offline chapter
- 7% Trail Sage / CTA and path
- 3% Supporting derived tones

### Section sequence

1. Light canvas header
2. Dark or photographic hero inside a rounded frame
3. Light Plan Together chapter
4. Meadow Sage Pack Together chapter
5. Brand Dark Ready Anywhere chapter
6. Light How It Works and editorial moment
7. Sage-led final CTA
8. Brand Dark footer

### Contrast rules

- Trail Sage button uses Background Dark text
- Warm Cream text is not used on Trail Sage for normal-size copy
- Warm Cream is used on Background Dark
- Meadow Sage is a supporting surface and hover color, not a dark replacement

## 7. Typography

### Display

Geist, weight 500–600.

The largest headings should feel open rather than heavy:

- Tight but not compressed letter spacing
- Balanced line breaks
- Lowercase sentence style preferred
- One visual idea per line

### Body

Inter, weight 400–500.

- Comfortable line height
- Maximum line length around 60–68 characters
- Plain language
- Dark neutral text rather than low-contrast grey

### Graphic copy behavior

Small route symbols or companion dots may enter a headline only after the Accomp symbol is approved. Until then, keep headlines typographic.

## 8. Layout Rhythm

### Desktop

- Sticky pill navigation within a wide page container
- Hero occupies most, not all, of the first viewport
- Major chapter introduction receives 40–60vh of breathing room
- Card rails may extend beyond the page container to suggest movement
- Dark chapter may pin visual content while copy advances

### Tablet

- Maintain headline impact but reduce empty vertical space
- Show roughly 1.5 cards to imply horizontal movement
- Avoid a desktop nav that wraps

### Mobile

- Logo, CTA and menu in one row
- Hero becomes portrait
- Feature cards become one-card focus
- Body copy is visible without waiting for scroll animation
- Use snap only as enhancement; native scrolling must remain usable
- Final CTA appears before a simplified footer

## 9. Hero Art Direction

### Composition

- Rounded dark frame on quiet light canvas
- Headline and CTA centered or slightly offset
- One phone mockup sits below the copy
- Two shared-path lines enter from opposite upper corners
- Lines cross behind the phone and leave as one coordinated route
- Low-contrast contour field creates depth
- Two companion dots remain visible near the route

### Motion cue

At rest, the frame already communicates the full idea. Motion only reveals the shared path and gently brings the phone into focus.

### Do not use

- Full-screen autoplay expedition video by default
- Phantom-like multicolor ribbons
- Mascot inserted inside the headline
- Generic mountain badge
- Floating feature chips without narrative meaning

## 10. Section Art Direction

### Plan Together

- Wide card rail
- Itinerary line travels across card boundaries
- Cards use Warm Cream, Trail Sand, Meadow Sage and Brand Dark accents
- Visual emphasis: trip, people, days

### Pack Together

- Checklist nodes expand from route nodes
- Assignments appear as rounded name labels
- Visual emphasis: shared responsibility, not task management

### Ready Anywhere

- Brand Dark full-width chapter
- Meadow Sage contours and route line
- Screen crop shows saved/offline state
- Visual emphasis: calm continuity when signal disappears

### Editorial Moment

- Real friendship photograph or a large quiet statement
- No fabricated quote marks, avatars or star rating

### Final CTA

- Large Trail Sage field or sage path on Brand Dark
- Headline remains the focal point
- One email field or one CTA, never both without a clear waitlist decision

## 11. Differentiation from Phantom

| Dimension | Phantom reference | Accomp direction |
|---|---|---|
| Primary world | Product/finance culture | Shared outdoor preparation |
| Signature | Ghost mascot | Shared path and companion dots |
| Primary color | Purple | Trail Sage |
| Hero energy | Bold, cinematic, high contrast | Calm, grounded, natural |
| Cards | Colorful product moments | Connected planning artifacts |
| Dark chapter | Security/product control | Offline continuity and reliability |
| Photography | Product-led / graphic-led | Genuine friendship and nature |
| Motion | Expressive brand animation | Purposeful route transformation |

## 12. Production Guardrails

- Every illustration must be original to Accomp
- Every photo needs a recorded source and commercial-use status
- A moodboard link does not grant production rights
- Do not recreate another app’s UI
- Do not show a feature screen that contradicts the real product
- Do not add mountain-circle, pine-tree or location-pin clichés to fill empty space
- Maintain readable content when images and motion are unavailable
