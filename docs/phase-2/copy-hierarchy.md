## 9. Mobile Copy Rules

- Do not remove essential meaning to fit a composition.
- Use the short chapter bodies above in the first view.
- Place extended copy inside cards, not behind a `Read more` interaction.
- Keep the hero lead to roughly 24–34 words.
- Do not orphan a one-word final line in H1/H2 at the 320px viewport.
- Keep CTA labels unchanged across breakpoints.
- FAQ answers may expand naturally; do not set a fixed height.
